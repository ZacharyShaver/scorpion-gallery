Scorpion

Scorpion is a tracked ground robot with a 360-degree pan-tilt camera head, built as a platform for embodied AI. These are the design pages and simulation results from its development: drag the 3D pages to turn them. Reviewers of the research paper "Checks That Can Fail" can start with the first section, which follows the paper.

For the paper's reviewers

These are the visuals behind the research plan "Checks That Can Fail", which asks how far AI-assisted design can be trusted when the checks that accept it can themselves be wrong. Its two-week pilot on this robot tested each kind of check with something outside it: planted faults for the CAD checks (H1), a simulator for the surrogate model (H2) and real hand drops for the simulator (H3). The items follow the paper in that order, each captioned with the claim it supports. The 3D pages further down can be turned with the mouse.
