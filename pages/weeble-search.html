What would count

  1. Primary: coins low, upright more often than E3's 16 of 30. That takes 24 of 30 or more (one-sided Fisher, p < 0.05).
  2. The simulation's own claim: 27 of 30 or more. Fewer means something it leaves out holds the body off its base: rolling resistance, the print's surface, the tape.
  3. Control: coins high lands upright less often than coins low.
  4. Known miss: real bodies flip more than modelled, so coins high should beat the 0.12–0.18 predicted. Direction only.
